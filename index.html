FAQ

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Everything on the site is free, with no payment and no ads.

I'm a complete beginner. Can I use it?

Yes. Take the level test first. If you're at Pre-A1 or A1, the plan starts with basic words, simple phrases and grammar from am / is / are.

Can I see meanings in Bangla?

Yes. The site is in English, but beginners from Bangladesh can switch on “Bangla help” on any learning page to see Bangla meanings of words and phrases, grammar explained in Bangla, and listening questions in Bangla.

Do I need to sign up?

No. Your progress is saved in your browser. If you log in with Google, it's also saved to your account, so you can continue on another phone or laptop.

How much time do I need each day?

About 30–40 minutes for the daily plan. If you miss a day, just continue where you stopped.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes, in any modern browser. Use headphones for listening. The speaking check works best in Chrome.
