30-day plan

Four weeks, from single sounds to full sentences

Each week builds on the last, so by Day 30 you're speaking whole sentences with clearer sounds and better rhythm.

Week 1 · Days 1–7

Consonants

The tricky consonant sounds that change meaning when they're wrong.

  • v / w
  • f / p
  • th
  • z / s
  • sh / s
Week 2 · Days 8–14

Vowels

Short and long vowels, so "ship" and "sheep" finally sound different.

  • ship / sheep
  • cat / bed
  • cup / car
  • diphthongs
  • schwa
Week 3 · Days 15–21

Endings & tricky sounds

Finish every word clearly and fix the sounds that trip most learners.

  • -ed
  • -s / -es
  • clusters
  • R / L
  • silent letters
Week 4 · Days 22–30

Full sentences

Stress, linking and intonation, so you sound natural, not word-by-word.

  • word stress
  • sentence stress
  • linking
  • intonation
  • pauses

FAQ

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. There's no payment, no account and no ads. Just open the app and start.

Do I need to sign up?

No. Your progress is saved in your own browser on the device you use.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. It works in any modern browser on phone, tablet or laptop. Use headphones for the best listening.

How much time do I need each day?

About 40 minutes, split into six short steps. If you miss a day, just continue where you left off.

Will my progress be lost if I clear my browser?

Clearing your browser data or switching devices resets your saved days. You can still jump to any day manually.
